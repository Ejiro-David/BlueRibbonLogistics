How do I book?

Tap any "Book on WhatsApp" button. The message opens already started, so you only fill in the pickup, the drop-off and the item.

How much does it cost?

Deliveries in Benin City start from ₦2,500. We send you the exact price on WhatsApp before pickup.

Can you collect payment for me?

Yes. The rider collects the payment from your customer at the door, and we send it on to you.

Do you deliver outside Benin City?

We take packages to the park and send them on by waybill to Lagos and other states.

Can you bring something back?

Yes. We can drop off one item and bring another back, or pick up from two places on one run.
