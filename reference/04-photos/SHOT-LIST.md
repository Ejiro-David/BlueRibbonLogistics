# Photo shot list

Real photos of your own riders are what make people trust a delivery business. A phone camera is enough. Take 10–20 photos for each shot and keep the best 2–3. Drop them into the matching folder here.

## The shots

| # | Folder | Shot | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `hero` | **Rider on the bike with a package,** stopped in a clear Benin street, facing slightly toward the camera, relaxed and ready. Take one upright (portrait) and one sideways (landscape). Leave empty space on one side for the headline. | Top of the page |
| 2 | `hero` | **Same rider against a plain light wall,** full body or from the knees up, holding a package. A plain background lets the rider be cut out onto white, like the food containers in Tip 01. | Hero, link-preview image |
| 3 | `handover` | **Rider handing a package over** at a gate, door or shop counter. Show the customer from behind or only their hands, unless they agree to be shown. | How it works |
| 4 | `packages` | **Neatly packed items:** sealed food containers, a wrapped parcel, a labelled package. Shoot from above or at a slight angle, on a clean surface. | Services, Why BRL |
| 5 | `vendors` | **Rider collecting from a shop** (clothes, make-up, frames), with the owner's permission. | For vendors |
| 6 | `riders` | **Each rider from the chest up,** smiling, with a plain background. Only take these if the rider agrees to appear on the site. | Why BRL |
| 7 | `riders` | **The bike up close:** clean bike, helmet, delivery box or bag. | Services, Why BRL |
| 8 | `handover` | **A phone in hand showing a WhatsApp booking chat.** Hide or blur every name, phone number and address. | How it works |

## Tips

- **Light:** shoot in the morning or late afternoon, with the sun behind the photographer. Avoid harsh midday sun.
- **Camera:** use the back camera, wipe the lens first, and hold still.
- **Clothes:** if there's a BRL shirt or bag, use it. If not, plain clothes with no other brand names.
- **Background:** keep other companies' logos and signboards out of the frame where you can.
- **Privacy:** never show a customer's face, phone number, address or package label without their permission.
- **Files:** send the original files, not WhatsApp-compressed copies. Use Google Drive or "send as document".
